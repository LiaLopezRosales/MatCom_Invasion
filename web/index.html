How to play

  • Move — point your mouse to steer the ship.
  • Shoot — click / hold to fire at enemies.
  • Pause — press Esc.
  • Lose a life — collide with an enemy.
  • Game over — lose all lives, or an enemy reaches the bottom.
  • Each mode has its own victory condition (see the cards).
